## Direct conclusions

- **Van der Pol at5–10%:** EDMD has exact support20/20, median coefficient error0.033604 and mean Score0.957598 (seedSE0.005362); TV has18/20,0.188339 and0.832485 (SE0.011440). EDMD has19/20 strict paired wins for both error and Score, with favorable mean differences for all five seeds.
- **Van der Pol at20%:** pooled EDMD median error0.170796 vsTV0.409199, but mean Score0.717775 vs0.729871 and exact support5/10 vs8/10. The trade-off reflects sampling: at s16 (observation interval0.08), EDMD wins error and Score in5/5 paired seeds (median errors0.112425 vs0.167322; meanScores0.868896 vs0.791051; exact4/5 each). At s64 (interval0.32), EDMD meanScore0.566654 vsTV0.668690 and exact1/5 vs4/5. Both conditions belong to the original prescribed grid.
- **Van der Pol at50%:** TV is better in both support and coefficient recovery: EDMD has zero Score wins in10 pairs, exact0/10 vs4/10, meanScore0.211463 vs0.546671.
- **Burgers:** TV and POD+TV have lower median errors and higher meanScores than POD-EDMD at every noise. At20–50%, POD-EDMD wins neither error nor Score in any of20 paired comparisons with TV.
- **Fisher–KPP:** at20–50%, POD-EDMD medianerror0.100748 and meanScore0.628414 exceed the parameter accuracy/Score of TV (0.758459,0.510996) and POD+TV (0.787082,0.530971); rawFD remains stronger in aggregate (0.095627,0.674671). Against TV, POD-EDMD errorwins7/10 andScorewins7/10 at20%; at50%, errorwins5/10 andScorewins6/10. POD+TV has the smallest medianerror at20% alone. Thus the broad-band ordering does not hold at every noise level.
- **Advection–diffusion:** at5%, POD-EDMD wins error and Score in10/10 pairs against raw-fieldTV. At10%, its s4 comparisons favor EDMD in5/5 but s16 only1/5. At20–50%, POD+TV is strongest in all three pooled metrics; EDMD's meanScore0.316719 is slightly above raw-fieldTV0.308422 even though its medianerror0.748441 is worse than0.673391. Metric-specific claims are necessary.
- **Lorenz–63:** EDMD has higher meanScore thanTV at every noise level but no exact-support recovery. At20–50%, rawFD's Score0.313462 exceeds EDMD0.295343 andTV0.259798. EDMD is not a general robustness improvement.
- **Exact support:** no arm recovers the complete Lorenz or PDE support anywhere in this5–50% comparison. Van der Pol is the sole complete-support success.

## Separate non-oracle1% evidence

All methods recover both Van der Pol equations in5/5 seeds. EDMD has lower coefficient error thanTV (dx medians0.001008 vs0.005308; dy0.004531 vs0.031298). All four Burgers arms recover the exact terms in5/5, with TV0.015588 andPOD+TV0.015832 slightly belowPOD-EDMD0.018629. For front-typeFisher, exact supports are RawFD0/5, POD-EDMD5/5, TV2/5, POD+TV5/5; coefficient medians are0.058688,0.010782,0.061225,0.007928. Thus EDMD's non-oracle advantage over raw-fieldTV does not imply superiority over matchedPOD+TV. This focused1% demonstration has different initial-condition/rank/settings from the high-noisegrid and must not be pooled with it.

## Reporting interpretation

Table means are record means on a fixedgrid. SE averages all conditions sharing a seed before taking SD/sqrt(5); it is not a claim about arbitrary new systems. Figure error summaries use medians, while Score uses means; their ordering need not coincide. Strict wins use the same observed system/noise/spacing/seed, with ties counted separately. No significance claim follows from boldface or standard-error bars.
